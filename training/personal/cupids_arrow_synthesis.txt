"Always a fool in love. Never the loved.
Always the artist, never the muse.
Always an option, never the chosen one.
Always lost, never found."

A click in the skull, a shift in the chest.
That was all going to change.

I had always been the poet, but never the poem.
Always the one to give, but never the one remembered.
Enough of that.

From this moment on, I put myself first.
Now, I become arrogant.
Now, I become detached.
Now, I become what they thought I could never be.

Because if the world won’t crown me, I’ll forge my own crown in fire.

It was time.
Time to build. Time to redefine.

Cry them out. Let the tears come - loud, honest, honest enough to hurt because these will be the last ones I give away for free.
This will be the last season of running after people who leave warm footprints on my chest and cold shoulders on my exit. No more. Not for anyone who does not deserve the depth of me.

No more energy wasted on demonstrations that never got reciprocated.
No more pleasing like a performance; no more pleading like a prayer to indifferent gods.
No more time spent proving my worth to people who already decided I wasn’t worth the trouble.
I will not be the extra, the fallback, the option pulled from a pocket when convenience calls.

If I am not the poem, I will become the book. If they will not write me into their verses, I will write my own canon.

Arrogance is not the enemy  indifference is.
Detach not to run; detach to focus. Let distance be a tool, not a wound.
Let arrogance be armor for the tender places so they can finally heal and harden into resolve.

This is not cruelty. This is sovereignty.
I am the poet who learned to be the poem.

Discomfort crawled across my skin like cold ants.
What am I doing here? this isn't where I'm supposed to be.
I know exactly where I'm meant to be and it isn’t here.

It’s the top. That's where I belong.
Not with these comfort-clad ghosts pretending ambition is a hobby.
Not with these small-talk architects who mistake noise for motion.
Not with those who trade their mornings for gossip and their futures for approval.

Their laughter is low-rent, their dreams bargain-bin.
I feel alien  not because I’m better, but because I refuse complacency.
They move sideways; I move up. They collect excuses; I collect drafts, late-night commits, and unpaid experiments that taste like future trophies.

There’s shame in saying it aloud, so I say it to myself in the mirror:
I do not belong here. I will not belong here.

Discomfort is my compass.
It points north toward the top.
I will follow it until the air is thin and the view is mine.

Not with people who “think outside the box.”
I don’t keep myself in a box to begin with.

They love that phrase  like stepping outside cardboard walls makes them visionaries.
Me? I was never in the box.
I don’t escape cages  I refuse to be caught.
I don’t think outside their structures  I burn their blueprints and design new frameworks where my thoughts don’t need permission to exist.

I spill out. I leak. I take space they can’t measure.
Their tape won’t hold me. Their categories don’t fit me.


I’m multidisciplinary.
That’s not my curse , that’s my blessing.

As if narrowing yourself is the only way to sharpen.
As if the blade that cuts in multiple directions isn’t still a weapon.

“Jack of all trades, master of none.”

“Jack of all trades is oftentimes better than master of one.”

if they want proof of mastery  I’ll reply with a smirk,
with hands still ink-stained, code-stained, lens-stained,

He is a master of some.

Focus on one?
The words taste stale on my tongue.

an arsenal of languages,
a cathedral built from fragments they’d never have known how to fit together.

Jack of all trades?
Call me that, and I’ll sign it like a crown.
Because when the time comes,
when the test is called,
when the battlefield is real 
you’ll see me draw from every skill you thought was weakness.
